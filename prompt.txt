## CORRECTION — PAYMENT MUST BE IMPLEMENTED ON QUOTES, NOT ORDERS

The previous implementation targeted **Order payment**, but that is NOT the requirement.

STOP and audit the implementation before making any further changes.

### Correct Business Requirement

The payment model being implemented is for **Request Quotes**.

When a customer receives an approved quote, they must have two payment options:

1. **Pay 50% deposit**

   * Customer pays 50% of the quote total.
   * The remaining 50% becomes outstanding.
   * The request may proceed through the approved post-payment workflow.

2. **Pay 100% upfront**

   * Customer pays the entire quote total.
   * No remaining quote balance exists.

After the work is completed, if the customer selected the 50% option, the remaining 50% must become payable.

### Critical Scope Rule

The implementation MUST operate on:

**Request → Quote → Quote Payment**

It MUST NOT implement this requirement through:

**Order → Order Payment**

Do not replace, redesign, or modify the existing product-order payment workflow unless the existing architecture explicitly requires a shared payment mechanism.

### Do NOT Guess

If the existing architecture does not clearly define when the remaining 50% becomes payable, STOP and report:

**UNRESOLVED — BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED**

Do not invent a lifecycle state or payment rule.

### Implementation Rules

* Do not move Quote payment logic into the Order domain.
* Do not alter product-order payment behavior.
* Do not introduce new lifecycle states unless already approved.

* Preserve existing Paystack integration.

* Ensure webhook processing updates the correct Quote-related payment record.
* Ensure customers cannot pay more than the outstanding quote balance through the application workflow.
